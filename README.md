Lumenfall is a neon top-down arena shooter: survive 10 escalating waves of voidborn, break the Warden bosses guarding waves 5 and 10, chain kills into a combo multiplier, and keep going in endless mode. WASD/arrows fly, mouse aims, hold click to fire, Space dashes through danger. Synthesised WebAudio sound, procedural glow art, and no libraries or network loads.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts 4173
# then open http://localhost:4173/
```

Or just open `game/index.html` in a browser (serve it over HTTP — ES modules need it).

**How to play:** WASD or arrow keys fly, mouse aims, hold the left mouse button (or Space) to fire, Space (or Shift / right-click) dashes through danger with brief invulnerability, P pauses, M mutes. Chain kills within 3 seconds to raise your combo up to x8. Grab hearts, shields, triple shot and overcharge. Clear wave 10 to win — then endless mode begins, with a boss every 5 waves.
