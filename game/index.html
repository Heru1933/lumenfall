Lumenfall

Survive 10 waves. Break the Warden. Keep the light.

How to play: WASD or arrow keys fly, mouse aims, hold the left mouse button (or Space) to fire, Space dashes through danger, P pauses, M mutes. Chain kills to raise your combo; grab hearts, shields, triple shot and overcharge. Bosses guard waves 5 and 10 — clear wave 10 to win, then endless mode begins.
